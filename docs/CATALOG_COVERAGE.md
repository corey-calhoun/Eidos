# Curated Catalog Coverage

**Status:** Local curated-core catalog

**Coverage target:** At least 75 validated records, followed by source-reviewed expansion batches

**Last review:** 2026-10-11

**Current core:** 79 validated records and 59 explained relationships

## Purpose

The built-in catalog is intended to be useful for governance discovery, policy planning, and source verification without claiming exhaustive coverage or permanent currency. It separates publisher material from Eidos editorial policies, controls, concepts, and relationships.

## Curated-core composition

The first expanded core targets meaningful depth across:

- frameworks;
- laws and regulations;
- contract clauses and publisher guidance;
- standards and evaluation criteria;
- maintained knowledge bases;
- control catalogs;
- reusable policies with Policy Playbooks;
- practical controls;
- foundational GRC concepts.

Every selectable industry context must have at least one directly tagged record. Every curated-core record must also have at least one explained relationship so discovery does not end at an isolated detail page. Cross-industry records remain available as broad starting points but do not substitute for dedicated sector coverage.

## Inclusion rules

A publisher record is included only when it has:

1. a stable Eidos identifier and slug;
2. an issuing authority and registered official host;
3. an official HTTPS source link;
4. a short Eidos paraphrase rather than reproduced protected text;
5. known version, revision, publication, effective-date, and jurisdiction context where verified;
6. bounded applicability language that does not decide whether an organization is legally or contractually in scope;
7. editorial review metadata.

Eidos-authored policies, controls, and concepts must remain machine-readably and visibly editorial. Every policy in the curated core includes a Policy Playbook, but playbooks do not track completion, evidence, findings, gaps, ownership assignments, due dates, scores, or compliance.

## Relationship rules

- `official` is used only when an authoritative publisher source explicitly supports the relationship and both endpoints are publisher records.
- `eidos_curated` provides orientation and never establishes equivalence, compliance, or an authoritative crosswalk.
- `unverified` remains a candidate and cannot be displayed as established.

Related publications remain separate when they have different authority or function. A law, regulation, contractual clause, implementation guide, standard, control catalog, policy, control, and concept must not be flattened into one record merely because they address the same topic.

## Current limitations

- The catalog is not exhaustive and does not guarantee that every source remains current after the recorded review date.
- Automated publisher monitoring, update alerts, editorial approval queues, and signed catalog releases are not implemented.
- Some publisher sites restrict automated retrieval. Those records contain only verified metadata, paraphrase, and an official link; Eidos does not reproduce protected standards.
- Industry tags are Eidos editorial classifications unless an authoritative source directly supports the classification.
- Selecting an industry or opening a record does not determine legal, regulatory, contractual, certification, or audit applicability.

## Expansion process

Future batches should be prioritized by user value and material gaps, then pass:

1. official-source and version review;
2. copyright and content-treatment review;
3. strict schema validation and duplicate detection;
4. relationship-authority review;
5. product-language and applicability review;
6. automated acceptance, browser checks, and independent Application Security review.
